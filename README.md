An entry for [The picture that tastes of one ingredient](https://make.fun/c/the-picture-that-tastes-of-one-20261122-2047) on make.fun.

One ingredient, one picture: a chunk of raw golden honeycomb, honey
dripping from it in a slow glistening thread, shot close on dark slate
under warm light. Nothing else on the plate — the honey is the whole
dish.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only, no network) validates
the bundled picture `asset.jpg` (1024x1024 JPEG, 205,268 bytes) and
writes it to `/output/entry.png`.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
